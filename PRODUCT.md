# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nguoi dung chinh la nhung nguoi thuong xuyen nghe nhac hoac audio tu YouTube va muon tai, quan ly, va nghe lai noi dung ngay tren may ca nhan. Ho su dung ung dung trong boi canh ca desktop web lan desktop dong goi, uu tien toc do thao tac, su quen thuoc, va kha nang kiem soat thu vien local cua rieng minh.

## Product Purpose

Nhac ton tai de bien viec lay audio tu YouTube thanh mot quy trinh gon va dang tin cay: nhap hoac dan link nhanh, tai audio ve may, quan ly thu vien local, nghe lai de dang, danh dau yeu thich, va sap xep noi dung theo album hoac playlist. Thanh cong cua san pham la giam ma sat giua luc nguoi dung muon luu mot noi dung va luc noi dung do nam gon trong thu vien ca nhan, san sang phat lai bat ky luc nao.

## Brand Personality

Warm, Focused, Reliable. Giong dieu va cam nhan tong the can tao su than thien, gan gui, tap trung vao noi dung audio, va cho thay ung dung la mot cong cu on dinh de dung hang ngay. Cam xuc uu tien la de chiu, ro rang, quen tay, va khong gay sao nhang khoi nhac, audio, va thao tac chinh.

## Anti-references

Khong theo huong SaaS marketing mau me hoac giao dien khoa truong. Tranh gradient tim xanh kieu AI generic. Tranh card long card, shadow nang, bo goc qua lon, layout chat va nhieu nut canh tranh su chu y. Khong chap nhan text contrast thap, co chu qua nho, hoac bieu dat thi giac lam giam kha nang doc va dieu huong.

## Design Principles

1. Nhanh vao viec: moi man hinh phai giup nguoi dung di tu link YouTube den audio local voi it ma sat nhat co the.
2. Thu vien la trung tam: dieu huong, metadata, favorites, albums, va queue phai lam ro cam giac so huu va kiem soat thu vien ca nhan.
3. Uu tien noi dung, khong uu tien trang tri: giao dien can am ap va co ban sac, nhung khong lam lu mo bai hat, player, va cac thao tac chinh.
4. Dang tin cay trong moi trang thai: loading, loi, empty state, va tac vu dang dien ra phai duoc truyen dat ro rang va de hieu.
5. Quen tay moi ngay: keyboard support, target du lon, va feedback ro giup ung dung hoat dong nhu mot cong cu nghe nhac dung thuong xuyen, khong phai demo UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Can dam bao contrast ro va de doc, ke ca trong cac bien the theme neu bo sung dark mode sau nay. Keyboard navigation phai ho tro cho search, player controls, va library list. Focus state can ro rang, click va tap target du lon cho play, download, favorite va cac thao tac chinh. Empty state phai ro khi chua co bai hat hoac chua co download. Loading va error state phai de hieu, dac biet khi tai audio that bai hoac link YouTube khong hop le.
